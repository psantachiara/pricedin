How to read this

Prices are daily closes adjusted for splits and dividends, updated each weekday evening from Yahoo Finance. Market value multiplies each close by today’s share count, so older values are approximate.

An announcement is placed on the first trading day on or after its date, or the day after if it came out after the market closed. Its move runs from the previous close to the close at the end of the reaction window.

“Against benchmark” subtracts the benchmark’s move over the same days, a simple way to separate a stock’s reaction from the market’s. Earnings, macro news and other stories still land on the same days, so a move shows what followed an announcement, not what it caused.

Which stocks an announcement touches is an editorial choice, recorded in data/events.json. The industry total combines only the companies selected above.
